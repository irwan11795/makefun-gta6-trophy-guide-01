⚠ One missable trophy — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy in the game that can be missed. Lucia invites you to meditate on the balcony at Jason’s house on a morning after the Down to Earth mission in Chapter 1 — that invitation is gone once Chapter 1 ends, and there is no mission replay and no New Game+. Take it the moment it is offered. The last of the 18 meditation spots needs Jason and Lucia together, after Chapter 2.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    Three phases, in this order. Story first, clean-up second, stranger strands last. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter (15 save slots) — there is no New Game+ and cheats disable trophies.

    1. Step 1 — The story 60–80 hours · 5 chapters

      Play the main missions through all five chapters. Every story trophy pops on its own as missions and chapters end — none of them can be missed. Two things to do while you play: take the balcony meditation in Chapter 1 the morning after Down to Earth (see the warning above), and scan vehicles and learn animals from the start, because those counts feed the 100% checklist later.

      Earns: all 12 story trophies + the final-mission gold.

    2. Step 2 — 100% completion clean-up about 100 hours after the story

      Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist category by category: 100 optional tasks, 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 34 of 35 smash ’n’ grabs, 50 of 91 stick-ups, $100k of narcotics, $100k of jewellery, $250k of marked bills, $200k of fenced vehicles, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater finds, 100 flamingos, 30 race wins, 42 gold shooting-range medals, 42 drone time trials, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of Bret’s target decks, 200 vehicles and 50 weapons found, 100 animals.

      Earns: A Long Road (gold) and most activity, robbery, collectible and money trophies.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and character finales 10–20 hours

      Finish the eight stranger strands that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and four more — Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy and Billy do not count here) and the final mission of each character strand: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone. These strands stay open after the story, so nothing here can be missed.

      Earns: Strangers & Freaks (silver) and the five character-finale trophies.

    100% checklist at a glance

    • 250 stunts for Cunning Stunts (300 for 100%)
    • 42 gold medals, 12 shooting ranges — The Shootist
    • 1 drone-race win at each of 14 locations — Top Fun
    • 20 different race wins — Earn Cash, Not Kudos
    • 3 of 18 card sets — Don’t Touch My Deck (15 for 100%)
    • 100 WAiNK photos — You WAiNKer!
    • 100 vehicles scanned — Whip It Good (200 for 100%)
    • 5 of 8 bank vaults — Maximum Withdrawal
    • 1 illegal operation per gang, 9 gangs — Pest Control
    • $250k marked bills laundered — Dime on the Dollar
    • 8 garages bought — Little Boxes
    • All of Leonida explored — The Goodtime State

    ⏰ Last chance — the one trophy you can lose

    There is exactly one missable trophy in the game. Every other trophy can be earned after the story ends. This is the only point of no return.

    Save manually at the start and end of every chapter (15 save slots). If you reach the end of Chapter 1 without the balcony meditation, that playthrough cannot earn the platinum — there is no mission replay and no New Game+.
